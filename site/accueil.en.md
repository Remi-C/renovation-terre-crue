Field notes from a homeowner renovating a cob (earth) house near Rennes, France. When a practical question has no
published answer, we try it, measure it with ordinary tools, and publish everything: the raw readings, the code that
rebuilds every number and figure, the photos, and the advice we think they support.

These are not laboratory tests. They are published to be discussed, repeated elsewhere and corrected: each topic says
what is measured, what is estimated, what is calculated and what is assumed.
